Studio profile / 品牌工作室简介

Essence as material. Story as form.

Founded by Aven Xu, SCARLET VINCI® operates through digital, physical, and conceptual formats. The studio collaborates with leaders in fragrance, cosmetics, and fashion. 由 Aven Xu 创立，工作内容覆盖香氛产品、视觉设计与概念叙事。

Core services Perfume design, perfumery studio, fragrance products
Location Based in Shanghai
Clients Fragrance, cosmetics, fashion, private commissions

Practice domains / 实践领域

Four ways to construct scent.

Each project begins with a material, an atmosphere, or a scene. The final form may be a perfume, a product system, a ritual, or a spatial memory. 从原料、氛围或场景开始，最终落到香水、产品系统、仪式或空间记忆。

01

Perfume Design

Formula direction, olfactory profile, and perfume identity for products and private briefs.

02

Perfumery Studio

Small-batch experiments, sample development, and fragrance material studies.

03

Fragrance Products

Commercial collections, packaging direction, and retail-ready fragrance systems.

04

Immersive Storytelling

Scent-led narratives for events, brand activations, exhibitions, and gifting.
